## Key findings

1. Biggest gap for video calls: Studio Effects voice focus. Packages exist in official repos (rnnoise, deepfilternet-ladspa) and EasyEffects is already installed. Cost: low.
2. Modern Standby is not matched. Linux suspend is s2idle with no network during sleep, and wake-on-notification is not possible. Mitigate with suspend-then-hibernate.
3. The NPU driver and OpenVINO are in CachyOS repos but not installed. Current NPU use is for small models only. NPU uses here are research, not daily value.
4. VA-API (iHD) decodes and encodes H.264, HEVC, VP9 and AV1. Good for OBS and ffmpeg. Resolve needs the NVIDIA dGPU.
5. Linux is ahead on ASUS controls: asusctl profiles, MUX via supergfxctl, panel overdrive, 80% charge limit and fan curves. Windows here has no Armoury Crate app.
6. Hello-style face unlock is possible with IR but needs AUR tools and PAM care. Fingerprint hardware is absent.
7. Gaps with no Linux equivalent: ambient-light auto-brightness (no driver), Auto HDR, NVIDIA Broadcast, RTX VSR, OLED pixel refresh (unverified).
